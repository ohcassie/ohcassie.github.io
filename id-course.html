Instructional Design: AI Fluency Course

Scoping, designing, and delivering an in-house AI fluency curriculum using ADDIE, from an enterprise-wide survey through two recurring vILT sessions with real participation growth.

DWG-01 · Analyze

Started with a survey, not an assumption

Rather than guessing what professional development the organization needed, the team surveyed the enterprise at the start of the year to scope out course topics directly from colleagues.

RESULT

75% of respondents said they wanted to increase their awareness of AI technologies, more than any other topic surfaced in the survey. That result is what scoped this course into existence.

DWG-02 · Design & Develop

From a skill gap to a delivery model

With the gap identified, the design had to answer two questions: what business outcome should this actually move, and what format would colleagues realistically show up for.

DESIGN

Anchor to business impact

The curriculum was built around four outcomes: increased productivity across teams, enhanced decision making and accuracy, cost savings through efficiency, and accelerated innovation and competitive advantage. Every module had to trace back to one of these, not just teach a tool for its own sake.

DEVELOP

Land on a repeatable format

The team landed on two vILT (virtual instructor-led training) sessions, each 1.5 hours, repeated throughout the year as a recurring lunch and learn series rather than a one-time launch.

DWG-04 · Implement

Delivered as a recurring series, not a one-off

Both sessions run repeatedly throughout the year as part of an ongoing lunch and learn series, rather than a single launch event. That format let colleagues opt in on their own timeline instead of requiring one mandatory rollout.
