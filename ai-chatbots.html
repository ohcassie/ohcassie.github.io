DWG-01 · Site Survey

The tools existed. The habit didn't.

The barrier here wasn't access. AI tools were already available across the organization. The barrier was skepticism and low adoption, the usual gap between a tool being technically available and people actually reaching for it in their day-to-day work.

01

AI tools were available but underused. Most colleagues had never tried them for anything beyond a novelty question.

02

The people team specifically had no visible example of AI doing real HR work, so it stayed abstract instead of practical.

03

A single broad, generic bot tends to underdeliver. Narrow, purpose-built bots needed a champion willing to build and prove them out first.

That last point is where this became personal rather than assigned. As an early adopter of new technology, the goal was to make the people team a visible example of what's possible with the tools already sitting in front of everyone, not to wait for a mandate.
